Research

从器件到知觉 From devices to percepts

视觉侧重远距离空间感知，触觉侧重近距离物理交互。课题组围绕这两条通道，开展智能传感器件、读出电路与神经形态信号处理研究，希望让机器人具备类人感知与交互能力，并为人类感知功能的重建与增强提供技术路径。核心问题是：高维多模态感知信息如何经器件获取、电路编码，转化为人可感知、机器人可使用的有效信息。 Vision spans space at a distance; touch mediates near-field physical interaction. We work across both channels—devices, readout circuits, and neuromorphic signal processing—to give robots human-like sensing and to restore or augment human perception. The core question is how high-dimensional multimodal signals can be acquired, encoded, and turned into information people can feel and robots can use.

01

触觉感知与电子皮肤 Tactile sensing & electronic skin

研究新型触觉传感器件与高密度阵列，以及多模态触觉信息的融合方法。现有器件在空间分辨率、灵敏度等参数上已接近甚至超过人体指尖机械感受器，但触觉信息如何编码、传递并转化为可被神经系统解读的信号仍不清楚，制约了从感知到感觉反馈的闭环。课题组将传感与反馈调控结合起来，发展面向智能机器人与神经假肢的仿生触觉系统，覆盖高密度压力成像、切向力与滑移、软硬与模量等模态。 We develop tactile devices and high-density arrays, with methods for multimodal fusion. Spatial resolution and sensitivity already approach or exceed fingertip mechanoreceptors, yet how tactile signals should be coded, transmitted, and made readable to the nervous system remains unclear—limiting closed-loop sensation. We couple sensing with feedback control for robotic and neuroprosthetic systems, spanning high-density pressure imaging, shear and slip, and stiffness/modulus modalities.

02

视觉感知与神经形态器件 Vision & neuromorphic devices

研究新型光电探测器件与神经形态视觉阵列。生物视网膜可在信号传出前完成对比度增强、运动检测等初级处理；现有器件难以在保持探测性能的同时实现「感—存—算」一体，这是仿生视觉的主要瓶颈之一。课题组从材料、器件与系统层面入手，发展面向机器视觉与人工视觉的仿生视觉感知系统。 We study photodetectors and neuromorphic vision arrays. Biological retinas perform early processing—contrast enhancement, motion detection—before signals leave the eye; most devices still struggle to keep detection performance while integrating sensing–memory–computing. From materials and devices to systems, we pursue bioinspired vision for machines and artificial sight.
