让机器与人重新获得感知。 Restoring perception for machines and people.

围绕视觉与触觉两条通道，从传感器件、读出电路到神经形态处理，构建可进入机器人与人体闭环的仿生感知系统。 We build bioinspired sensing systems across vision and touch—from devices and readout circuits to neuromorphic processing—for closed loops in robots and humans.

Research

从器件到知觉 From devices to percepts

核心问题：高维多模态感知信息如何经器件获取、电路编码，转化为人可感知、机器人可使用的有效信息。 Core question: how high-dimensional multimodal sensory signals can be acquired, encoded, and turned into information people can feel and robots can use.

01

触觉感知与电子皮肤 Tactile sensing & electronic skin

高密度压力成像、切向力与滑移、软硬与模量等触觉模态；关注编码、传递与反馈，面向智能机器人与神经假肢的仿生触觉闭环。 High-density pressure imaging, shear and slip, stiffness and modulus sensing—with coding, transmission, and feedback for robotic and neuroprosthetic tactile loops.

02

视觉感知与神经形态器件 Vision & neuromorphic devices

新型光电探测与神经形态视觉阵列，推动高性能光探测与「感—存—算」一体处理，面向机器视觉与人工视觉。 Photodetectors and neuromorphic vision arrays that couple high-performance detection with sensing–memory–computing for machine and artificial vision.
